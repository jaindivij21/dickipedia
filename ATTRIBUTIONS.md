# Attributions & data licenses

dickipedia aggregates only **public records about public officials' public conduct**.

| Source                                                                  | Used for                                                                                                                                                                                 | License                                        | Re-host?                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Election Commission of India (via opencity.in CKAN)                     | results: winner, runner-up, margin, vote share, NOTA                                                                                                                                     | Public Domain                                  | Yes                                                                              |
| PRS Legislative Research (`prsindia.org/mptrack` + `Vonter` mirror)     | attendance, questions, debates, PMBs, by-ministry; **deep: per-MP vs national/state averages + debate titles**; portraits (hotlinked)                                                    | ODbL-1.0 (share-alike)                         | Yes — **attribute PRS Legislative Research**; portraits hotlinked, not re-hosted |
| Lok Sabha Secretariat (`sansad.in` + `api_ls`)                          | identity, **deep contact: public emails, phones, postal address, social links**, profile link                                                                                            | Facts only; portraits/bios all-rights-reserved | Facts + deeplink only                                                            |
| ADR / MyNeta / National Election Watch (`LokSabha2024` + `ls2014`)      | criminal cases (**pending vs convicted, court, IPC sections**), assets (**movable/immovable breakdown**), **sources of income + ITR, govt contracts**, wealth time-series 2014→2019→2024 | Non-commercial                                 | Display + attribution; open layer OCR'd from ECI affidavit PDFs                  |
| Ministry of Statistics & PI — MPLADS eSAKSHI                            | local-area development fund use                                                                                                                                                          | GODL-India                                     | Yes — attribute MoSPI                                                            |
| ECI / SBI electoral-bond disclosure (SC-ordered, ADR v UoI 15 Feb 2024) | party funding (party-level only)                                                                                                                                                         | Public-domain facts                            | Yes                                                                              |
| Wikidata / Wikimedia Commons                                            | dynastic relations, cross-IDs, portraits; **party election symbols (SVG/PNG)**                                                                                                           | CC0 / per-file CC (see per-symbol list below)  | Yes — per-file, with attribution                                                 |
| Wikipedia (English)                                                     | one-paragraph leader **bio extract** (descriptive prose; the only non-registry source; excluded from scoring)                                                                            | CC BY-SA 4.0                                   | Yes — extract + link, attributed CC BY-SA                                        |

**Computed inferences** are arithmetic over the sourced facts above (e.g. asset growth vs declared income, attendance percentile). They author no accusation, characterise no person, and each cites the registries it derives from. They are a derived projection (`scores.score_type='inferences'`), never a primary fact.

**Aggregated dataset:** ODbL-1.0 (PRS share-alike binds the whole DB). **Photos:** own per-file licenses, never relicensed. **Bios:** Wikipedia CC BY-SA, attributed. **Code:** MIT. **UGC:** separate contributor terms. Each field in the canonical table carries a per-field provenance + license column.

## Party election symbols

Each party's ECI-allotted election symbol is re-hosted from Wikimedia Commons under its own open licence (recorded per-party in `data/canonical/parties.json` as `symbol_source_url` / `symbol_license` / `symbol_author`). Parties without a confidently-verified Commons symbol render an initials fallback. MP portraits are **not** re-hosted — they are hotlinked from sansad.in / PRS mptrack / MyNeta under those sources' own terms.
